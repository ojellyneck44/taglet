          Link a device

          Scan this with your other phone or computer’s camera, or open taglet.net there and type the code.

            Files pass through Taglet’s relay encrypted with a key made from the code. The relay can’t open them, and keeps nothing.

            New builds

            These look like new versions of songs you’ve already tagged. Replacing keeps each song’s title, artists, number, album and cover, and swaps in the new audio.

              After you export, save over the old files in the same folder. Spotify then keeps one copy of each song instead of adding a duplicate.

              Share album

              Anyone with the link can play the songs and download them, tagged and ready for Spotify. Nobody needs an account.

              Songs are encrypted on this device before they’re uploaded, and the key is part of the link, so Taglet can’t open them. Links last 30 days, and you can stop sharing at any time.

                Quality

                Encoder delay and padding are always handled: other formats are decoded gap-free and every MP3 keeps its gapless header. MP3s you add are only re-tagged, never re-encoded, unless you trim.

                Why MP3 and not WAV? Spotify plays WAV and FLAC on some computers but ignores their tags and art, and phones skip them. MP3 is the one format every Spotify app reads fully.
