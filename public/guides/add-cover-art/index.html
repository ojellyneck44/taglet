How to add album cover art to MP3 files

Cover art lives inside the file. Embed it once and it shows up in every player, on every device, even offline.

What makes a good cover

  • Square. Albums are shown as squares. Taglet’s crop tool lets you pick the part of a non-square image to keep.
  • At least 600 × 600 pixels, ideally 1000 × 1000. Bigger images only make the file larger.
  • JPEG or PNG. Taglet embeds a JPEG, which every player supports.

Steps

  1. Open Taglet and add the songs of one album.
  2. Click the cover square, then choose an image, or drop one in. If your songs already contain a cover, Taglet finds it and offers to use it for the whole album.
  3. Crop it and confirm.
  4. Export. The cover is written into every MP3 of the album.

Where to find cover art

The album’s Bandcamp page, the artist’s website, or the store page where you bought the music usually have a clean, large image. If you’re adding a track from YouTube or SoundCloud through Taglet’s paste-a-link feature, its thumbnail comes with it and opens in the crop tool straight away. Only use artwork you have the right to use.

Why a cover might not show

  • Some players cache old artwork. Restart the app, or remove and re-add the song.
  • Spotify’s desktop app sometimes shows one cover for all local songs by the same artist; the art in each file is still correct and shows on phones.
  • The image was too large. Very big files are ignored by some car stereos, so stick to around 1000 pixels.
