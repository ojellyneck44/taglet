# Product

Inferred from Stevie's brief (Taglet Prompt.pdf). Assumptions are marked.

- **What:** a web app (installable PWA) that turns any audio into tagged MP3s ready for Spotify Local Files, with album grouping that holds together in Spotify.
- **For:** people with music Spotify doesn't have: demos, DJ edits, rips, bootlegs, their own bounces. Mostly 16–30 (assumption), on a phone and a laptop.
- **Core task:** drop files, then fix titles, artists and cover, see "Ready for Spotify", and export.
- **Promise:** files never leave the device. The one exception, Link a device, is end-to-end encrypted.
- **Tone:** Apple-ecosystem calm, dark, a little playful (the tag on a string), and trustworthy above all.
- **Surfaces:** `/` the app (Operate mode), `/spotify/` guide (Read), `/privacy/` (Read), `/brand/` style sheet (Read).
- **Not now:** native apps, online metadata lookup, accounts.
- **Also:** a portfolio case study for Stevie's internship applications, so the craft is part of the brief.
